Project AIM4SafeBaby

We sincerely thank all participating hospitals and clinical teams for their support and collaboration in this research initiative. This multi-institutional study is being conducted in partnership with the University of East Anglia and participating NHS organisations.

The study has received approval from the NHS Health Research Authority and is partly funded by Innovate UK, reflecting its potential to advance clinical research and improve healthcare outcomes.

We are grateful to all collaborating sites for their commitment to supporting high-quality research and contributing to the generation of robust evidence in this field.
